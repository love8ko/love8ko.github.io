How it works

1

Start from what you already recite

Al-Fatiha first, then every other word of your prayer and the short chapters you recite. Pick your own wording of the tashahhud.

2

Listen and see each word

A well-known reciter, word by word highlighted. Tap any word to see what it means right there, with a short explanation and its sources.

3

Recall it without the translation

A short question, then the Arabic alone. What you mark comes back tomorrow, then in three days, then in a week.

What’s inside

Your whole salah

Al-Fatiha, from the opening takbir to the closing greeting, and 15 short chapters often recited after Al-Fatiha.

A minute before prayer

One quiet daily reminder at the time you choose, to recall one phrase before you stand to pray. No streaks.

Ask about any word

Questions under each lesson are answered from the lesson, with sources. Optional AI answers stay within your lesson’s material.

A phrase on your Lock Screen

Each day a phrase you’ve studied and its meaning, under the clock or among your apps. Tap it for a one-minute recall.

Your own route

Lessons with your name on them, in your order, continuing exactly where you stopped.

For the whole family

One plan covers your family with Family Sharing. No ads, anywhere.

Calm and private

  • No account and no ads. Your progress, saved phrases and conversations stay on your iPhone.
  • AI answers are off until you turn them on; you can see exactly what is shared before you agree.
  • Arabic from the Tanzil Quran text, recitation by Mishary Rashid Alafasy, explanations based on classical commentary and lexicons — each lesson shows its sources.
