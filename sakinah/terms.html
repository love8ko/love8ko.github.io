2. Content

The Arabic text of the Quran is the Tanzil Uthmani text (tanzil.net), copied without changes. The English translation line is by M. M. Pickthall (1930). Recitations are by Mishary Rashid Alafasy, streamed from Quran.com’s audio service. The texts of the formulas of the prayer come from hadith collections named in each lesson. Explanations, questions and prepared answers are written by Sakinah and checked by a qualified teacher; lessons still in review say so on their Sources screen. The Arabic font is Amiri Quran (SIL Open Font License).

AI answers in Ask are written automatically from the lesson’s material and can be imperfect. Each answer shows the sources it relies on.

For questions of practice, ask a scholar or teacher you trust.
