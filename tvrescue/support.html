Common questions

TVRescue can't find my TV.

  • Turn the TV on (not standby) and make sure your iPhone and the TV are on the same Wi-Fi network. Guest networks and some mesh or office networks keep devices apart.
  • Allow Local Network access: iPhone Settings → TVRescue → Local Network. Without it the app cannot see any TV.
  • Some TVs need network control turned on once (for example "Network debugging" on Fire TV and Android TVs without Google). The app shows where.
  • Still nothing? Tap the chat at the bottom of the setup screen, or take a photo of the sticker on the back of the TV so we know the exact model.

The remote stopped working.

Tap Fix on the remote. TVRescue looks for your TV again, reconnects and asks you to allow it on the TV if needed. If the TV changed its software, a fix can arrive without an app update.

Do I need an IR bridge?

Only for air conditioners and TVs without Wi-Fi. TVRescue works with BroadLink RM4 mini and RM4 pro bridges (and compatible RM4C and RM mini 3 models), sold separately by other shops and not included in the subscription. The app shows where to buy one in your country, and you can try the remote preview before you buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → TVRescue. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I have a new iPhone.

Open TVRescue with the same Apple Account and tap Restore purchases in Settings. Your TVs come back through iCloud Keychain if it is turned on.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete data on this iPhone removes your saved TVs and connection keys (on all your devices that share iCloud Keychain) and the diagnostics on this iPhone. To delete reports already sent to us, write to us from the app so we can find them by your installation ID. See the Privacy Policy.
