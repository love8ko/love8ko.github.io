Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

TVRescue ("the App") turns your iPhone into a remote for TVs and compatible air conditioners. It is developed and published by Valerii Loveiko ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the App keeps on your iPhone, what it sends to outside services and why, how long anything is kept, and the choices you have.

Summary: No account, no ads, no tracking. Your TVs, rooms and connection keys stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud Keychain; we cannot see them. Controlling your TV happens inside your home network. Only with your consent, photos you choose and your chat questions go through our server to AI services that read the photo or write an answer; our server does not keep them. Connection reports are sent only if you turn them on. Purchases are handled by Apple and RevenueCat.

1. Data stored on your iPhone

  • Your TVs and air conditioners: names, rooms, brand and model, network addresses, the hardware address used to wake a TV, and the keys a TV gives the App when you allow it.
  • Remote settings, your consent choices with their date, and the reminder you set when you order an IR bridge.
  • If iCloud Keychain is on, your list of TVs and their keys are synced to your other devices through your own Apple Account, end-to-end encrypted by Apple. We do not receive this data.

Settings → Delete data on this iPhone removes your TVs and keys (on all your devices that share iCloud Keychain) and the diagnostics on this iPhone. Deleting the App removes what is stored on this iPhone.

2. Your home network

To find and control your TV, the App looks for devices on your Wi-Fi (Bonjour, SSDP and the TV's own network ports) and sends commands directly to the TV or IR bridge. This traffic stays in your home network and does not go to us. iOS asks for Local Network permission the first time.

3. Data sent to outside services

3.1 AI setup help (with your consent)

On the "Almost there" screen you agree that AI helps you set up. You can withdraw this later in Settings; the App keeps working with Wi-Fi discovery, on-device text reading and manual model choice.

  • Photos you choose (the sticker on the TV, its remote or box, or a photo of the TV screen when you ask for help) are sent to our server, which passes them to OpenAI to read the brand, model or what the screen shows. Our server does not store the photos. OpenAI processes them under its own terms and privacy policy. A photo of the back of a TV can contain a serial number or network address; you can cover them or choose the model by hand.
  • Chat questions (up to 600 characters) and the setup context (brand, model, the step you are on, the connection error) are sent to our server. Before anything leaves the server, email addresses, IP and hardware addresses and long numbers are removed from the text. The server first looks for a prepared answer; if there is none, it asks DeepSeek, an AI service operated from the People's Republic of China, to write a short answer from our notes. Our server does not store the text of questions or answers; it logs only lengths, outcomes and identifiers for abuse limits. AI answers are labelled and may be wrong.
  • Check my connection (only when you tap it): the setup step, error code, the TV's open ports, the recipe used, how many devices were found, whether Local Network is allowed, and up to 30 recent connection events. No PIN, keys, Wi-Fi name, IP or hardware addresses or room names. Kept for 30 days.
  • Send to the team / Didn't help (only when you tap it): the text you send and the setup context, kept for 30 days to answer you and fix the problem. Our team may be notified through a private Telegram chat.

3.2 Connection reports (optional)

Only if you turn them on: anonymous events about setup and connections (TV brand and model, setup stages, error codes, which screens were shown), with a random installation identifier (a one-way hash). Requests to support a new TV model (brand, model, ports and your note) are sent to our team. We use them only to fix and improve connections. Turning reports off stops new sending and clears what is waiting to be sent; reports already sent are deleted on request.

3.3 Catalogs

The App downloads connection recipes, setup answers and the IR bridge shop list from our server. These requests contain no personal data.

3.4 Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple; we never see your name or payment details. The App uses RevenueCat to know whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat creates a random ID for your installation and receives purchase information (product, dates, trial and renewal status, Family Sharing) and technical details such as device model, iOS version, App version and region. Our server sends that random ID to RevenueCat to check whether AI help is included.

3.5 Our server

Cloudflare runs our server. To protect it from abuse it keeps daily request counters keyed by the random ID or a one-way hash of your IP address. Cloudflare's own service logs are kept for a few days. We do not use IP addresses to identify you or your location.

3.6 IR bridge shops

Links to shops and web search open in Safari or the shop's app. We do not send them any data about you and we do not use affiliate tracking.

3.7 Write to us

"Write to us" opens your mail app with the App version, iOS version, language, your installation ID and a connection report attached, so we can help. You can remove them before sending. We keep your email only to answer you.

5. What we do not do

  • No ads, no advertising or analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data for marketing.
  • No account and no email sign-up.
  • We do not use your photos or questions to train AI models.

6. Your choices and rights

You can withdraw AI help or connection reports at any time in Settings → Data and consent, and delete data as described above. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to processing. Write to loveykovl@gmail.com, ideally from the App so we can find your data by its installation ID. We answer within 30 days. Data is processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change, and important changes will be shown in the App. Contact: Valerii Loveiko, loveykovl@gmail.com.
