2. What the App can and can't do

  • The App controls TVs through the network features their makers provide. Which TVs connect and which buttons work depend on the brand, model, software version and your network. A TV update can change or stop this; we work on fixes, but cannot promise that every TV or button will work.
  • Your iPhone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network, and some TVs ask you to confirm on the TV or turn on a network setting once.
  • Air conditioners and infrared-only TVs need a Wi-Fi IR bridge (for example BroadLink RM4 mini or RM4 pro). The bridge is a separate product sold by other companies, is not included in the subscription, and is covered by its seller's terms and warranty. An infrared signal does not confirm that the device reacted; check the device itself.
  • Photo recognition and the AI chat can make mistakes. AI answers are labelled and are not checked by a person before you see them.
  • Do not rely on the App for anything safety-critical.
