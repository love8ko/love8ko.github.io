Privacy Policy

CrySnap: Baby Cry Translator

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Summary: CrySnap listens for eight seconds only to confirm your baby is crying. That check runs on your iPhone, and the recording is deleted straight after — it is never uploaded or kept. Questions you send to an AI guide travel as text only. No account is required. No ad trackers. Subscription analytics are handled by RevenueCat.

1. Overview

CrySnap is a parenting companion for parents of newborns. When your baby cries, the app listens for eight seconds to confirm the crying (using Apple's on-device sound analysis) and then gives you timed soothing steps built from your baby's age, last feed and time awake. It does not try to read the cause of a cry from the sound. Three AI guides (a pediatric nurse, a sleep coach and a lactation guide) answer questions, soothing sounds play on the device, and you can create a two-week summary of your own logs for your pediatrician. A Voice Mode lets you ask a question by speaking. CrySnap is a parenting companion. It is not a medical device, not a diagnostic tool, and not a substitute for professional medical advice.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following records are stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework. With your AI permission, relevant text and profile details can also be sent as described in section 3. Audio remains on your device:

  • Crying episodes you log (time, the soothing steps you tried, what helped, whether crying was detected) — not the audio
  • Last feed and wake times you tap in, and the pediatrician summary you create
  • Baby profile (name, age in weeks, weight if you choose to enter it)
  • Chat conversations with the in-app expert personas
  • Onboarding completion status, disclaimer acceptance, notification preferences
  • Soothing sounds last-used / favourites, sleep timer preferences
  • Recent cry events (used by the optional D+1 reminder if you opt in to it)

No account registration or email address is required. Optional profile details and information you type can include personal or sensitive information. RevenueCat creates an anonymous app user ID for purchases and access checks; this ID is also sent to our proxy to choose the correct access allowance.

Your baby's cry is never uploaded or kept. The eight-second recording is checked on your iPhone and deleted as soon as the check finishes. We have no upload path for audio.

3. Data Shared With Third-Party AI Services

The three guides in CrySnap (Sarah, Emma and Linda) are AI. Their answers are written by a third-party AI service, not by CrySnap and not by a person. Nothing is sent to an AI service until you allow it. Right after you subscribe — and again before your first question if you skipped it — the app shows a screen that lists what will be sent and who receives it, and asks you to choose “Allow AI answers” or “Not now”. You can withdraw that permission at any time in Settings → AI answers; after that the app sends nothing to any AI service.

Safety, usage limits and voluntary reports

Our proxies filter unsafe input and answers. Quota records use hashed references, not conversation text. In version 2.1, hold an AI answer and choose Report answer to send its answer ID, selected category and app version. Reports also use an anonymous app reference to investigate reports and block abuse. Reports from all regions, including the China AI route, go to our Cloudflare feedback service. No conversation text is included. Hashed quota counters and reports are scheduled for cleanup after seven UTC days. Entitlement status may be cached briefly to verify access.

AI permission is recorded separately from your purchase, with its version, decision time and purpose choices. Choosing Not now leaves local plans, history and sounds usable. Withdrawing permission stops new AI requests and cancels in-flight app requests; it does not delete data already processed by providers.

Apple speech recognition

Voice Mode converts a spoken question to text with Apple Speech Recognition — on the device when the device and language support it, otherwise by Apple under Apple's privacy terms. Only the resulting text is sent on, and only as part of an AI request described above. Voice Mode replies are spoken on the device with Apple AVSpeechSynthesizer.

4. Subscriptions

Subscription purchases are managed by RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service. RevenueCat receives:

5. Analytics and Tracking

CrySnap uses no advertising networks or cross-app tracking SDKs. RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription events for subscription analytics. Local activation records are kept on your phone; no separate general analytics SDK is integrated.

Only Apple's built-in crash diagnostics are collected if you have them enabled. You can disable these in your device Settings under Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

6. Notifications (Optional, Off by Default)

Reminders are switched on individually in Settings. They are local notifications scheduled on your device; we do not use a push provider, and no notification content is generated on a server.

7. Children's Privacy

CrySnap is rated 4+ and is intended for use by adult parents and caregivers. The app briefly records sound to confirm crying, which may capture an infant's voice — that audio is checked on the device, deleted immediately, and never uploaded. Parents may choose to provide a baby's age, feeding and health context for AI answers after granting permission. Those text details are processed as described above; the app is not intended for children to use independently. You can withdraw AI permission and delete local care records in Settings.

8. Medical Disclaimer

CrySnap is a parenting companion app. It is NOT a medical device, NOT a diagnostic tool, and NOT a substitute for professional medical advice, examination, diagnosis, or treatment.

CrySnap does not identify the cause of a cry and does not diagnose colic, reflux, GERD or any other medical condition; its soothing plans are built from your baby's age and routine. The chat personas (Pediatric Nurse, Sleep Coach, Lactation Consultant) provide general parenting guidance grounded in published clinical guidelines (AAP, CDC, La Leche League) and explicitly redirect any diagnostic questions to your pediatrician.

For any of the following — call your local emergency number (the app shows it for your region, for example 911 in the US, 112 in the EU, 999 in the UK, 120 in mainland China) or your pediatrician immediately:

  • Fever in a baby under 3 months (rectal temperature ≥100.4°F / 38°C)
  • Lethargy or unresponsiveness
  • Blue lips or fingertips (cyanosis)
  • Difficulty breathing, grunting, or laboured breathing
  • Signs of dehydration (sunken fontanelle, no wet diapers for 8+ hours)
  • Suspected ingestion of a household substance — call your local poison control centre or emergency number

Always trust your parental instinct; if something feels wrong, contact your pediatrician.

9. Pediatric Care Disclaimer

CrySnap suggestions are not a substitute for a pediatrician's examination. For persistent crying that does not respond to feeding, changing, or soothing for over an hour; for any change in feeding, sleeping, or behaviour patterns that concerns you; or for any medical question — please contact your pediatrician or a registered children's nurse. The chat personas inside the app will say the same thing.

10. Data Deletion

Care records are stored on your phone. In version 2.1, Settings → Delete data on this iPhone removes local history, baby profiles, chat, soothing sessions and team memory, and withdraws AI permission. Deleting the app also removes its local records. Neither action cancels an Apple subscription or deletes purchase records or data already sent to AI providers. Contact us for requests concerning report or purchase records; provider retention policies continue to apply.

You can also clear your team chat memory at any time via Settings → Reset Team Memory without deleting the app.
