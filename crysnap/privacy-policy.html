Privacy Policy

CrySnap: Baby Cry Translator

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Summary: CrySnap listens for eight seconds only to confirm your baby is crying. That check runs on your iPhone, and the recording is deleted straight after — it is never uploaded or kept. Questions you send to an AI guide travel as text only. No account is required. No analytics. No ad trackers.

1. Overview

CrySnap is a parenting companion for parents of newborns. When your baby cries, the app listens for eight seconds to confirm the crying (using Apple's on-device sound analysis) and then gives you timed soothing steps built from your baby's age, last feed and time awake. It does not try to read the cause of a cry from the sound. Three AI guides (a pediatric nurse, a sleep coach and a lactation guide) answer questions, soothing sounds play on the device, and you can create a two-week summary of your own logs for your pediatrician. A Voice Mode lets you ask a question by speaking. CrySnap is a parenting companion. It is not a medical device, not a diagnostic tool, and not a substitute for professional medical advice.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only using Apple's SwiftData framework and never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it:

  • Crying episodes you log (time, the soothing steps you tried, what helped, whether crying was detected) — not the audio
  • Last feed and wake times you tap in, and the pediatrician summary you create
  • Baby profile (name, age in weeks, weight if you choose to enter it)
  • Chat conversations with the in-app expert personas
  • Onboarding completion status, disclaimer acceptance, notification preferences
  • Soothing sounds last-used / favourites, sleep timer preferences
  • Recent cry events (used by the optional D+1 reminder if you opt in to it)

No account is required. No user profile, name, email, or identifying information is collected by CrySnap.

Your baby's cry is never uploaded or kept. The eight-second recording is checked on your iPhone and deleted as soon as the check finishes. We have no upload path for audio.

3. Data Sent to External Services

CrySnap connects to the following services to provide chat and voice features. No provider key is stored in the app. Which chat service handles your question depends on your App Store country and device region, not on the app language:

  • Outside mainland China: DeepSeek, through our Cloudflare Worker proxy (crysnap-api.veribag.workers.dev).
  • Mainland China (China App Store, or device region set to China mainland): Qwen on Alibaba Cloud Model Studio, through our Alibaba Cloud Function Compute service in Beijing. Requests from mainland China are never sent to the service above, and requests from elsewhere are never sent here.
  • Either way, a request contains only your typed or voice-transcribed message and a small context block (baby age, the current plan step, time of day). No audio, photos, name, email address or location is included, and our proxies do not log the content of messages.
  • Apple Speech Recognition — Voice Mode converts a spoken question to text. Recognition runs on-device when supported by the device and language; otherwise Apple's standard speech-recognition service may process the audio under Apple's privacy terms. CrySnap sends only the resulting transcript, not voice audio, to our chat Worker.

Voice Mode replies are spoken back to you using Apple AVSpeechSynthesizer, which runs entirely on-device. No text-to-speech audio is generated on a server.

No personal information, device identifier, contact data, or location is sent to these services.

4. Subscriptions

Subscription purchases are managed by RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • An anonymous device identifier generated by RevenueCat
  • Subscription status (active, in the 7-day free trial, or expired)
  • Product identifier of the purchased tier (Weekly / Annual)

RevenueCat does not receive any cry scans, chat messages, baby profile data, or audio. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for details.

5. Analytics and Tracking

CrySnap does not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs.

Only Apple's built-in crash diagnostics are collected if you have them enabled. You can disable these in your device Settings under Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

6. Notifications (Optional, Off by Default)

Reminders are switched on individually in Settings. They are local notifications scheduled on your device; we do not use a push provider, and no notification content is generated on a server.

7. Children's Privacy

CrySnap is rated 4+ and is intended for use by adult parents and caregivers. The app briefly records sound to confirm crying, which may capture an infant's voice — that audio is checked on the device, deleted immediately, and never uploaded. We do not knowingly collect any data about a child that is uploaded off-device. If you have concerns about your baby's data, deleting the CrySnap app permanently removes everything from your device.

8. Medical Disclaimer

CrySnap is a parenting companion app. It is NOT a medical device, NOT a diagnostic tool, and NOT a substitute for professional medical advice, examination, diagnosis, or treatment.

CrySnap does not identify the cause of a cry and does not diagnose colic, reflux, GERD or any other medical condition; its soothing plans are built from your baby's age and routine. The chat personas (Pediatric Nurse, Sleep Coach, Lactation Consultant) provide general parenting guidance grounded in published clinical guidelines (AAP, CDC, La Leche League) and explicitly redirect any diagnostic questions to your pediatrician.

For any of the following — call your local emergency number (the app shows it for your region, for example 911 in the US, 112 in the EU, 999 in the UK, 120 in mainland China) or your pediatrician immediately:

  • Fever in a baby under 3 months (rectal temperature ≥100.4°F / 38°C)
  • Lethargy or unresponsiveness
  • Blue lips or fingertips (cyanosis)
  • Difficulty breathing, grunting, or laboured breathing
  • Signs of dehydration (sunken fontanelle, no wet diapers for 8+ hours)
  • Suspected ingestion of a household substance — call your local poison control centre or emergency number

Always trust your parental instinct; if something feels wrong, contact your pediatrician.

9. Pediatric Care Disclaimer

CrySnap suggestions are not a substitute for a pediatrician's examination. For persistent crying that does not respond to feeding, changing, or soothing for over an hour; for any change in feeding, sleeping, or behaviour patterns that concerns you; or for any medical question — please contact your pediatrician or a registered children's nurse. The chat personas inside the app will say the same thing.

10. Data Deletion

All cry scans, chat messages, baby profile, and preferences are stored locally on your device. Deleting the CrySnap app permanently removes this data from your device. No server-side record of your scans, chats, or baby data exists to be deleted. Your RevenueCat anonymous ID resets when you reinstall.

You can also clear your team chat memory at any time via Settings → Reset Team Memory without deleting the app.

11. Regional Compliance

CrySnap complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Economic Area, the UK Data Protection Act for UK users, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) for California residents. Because we do not collect personally identifying information, most data-subject rights (access, rectification, portability, deletion) are satisfied automatically — you already hold full control over your data on your device.

The emergency number shown in the app is chosen from your device's region. The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline and Postpartum Support International are shown only in the US and Canada.
