1.6 · Design principles — locked, apply across the app

  • Warm-severity verdict triad — never clinical red. NAFLD / MASH users catastrophize («fatty liver → cirrhosis → cancer»). Severity reads teal → amber → clay, never a stoplight red.
  • Night mode = the AI Coach surface. The 2am-anxious user — Nick after a scary lab, Robert checking his recovery — gets a calm dark Coach automatically 22:00–06:00.
  • Hopeful, non-judgmental framing. Especially for Robert: alcohol-related liver disease is stigmatised and often hidden from family. Use «recovery», «healing», «rebuild» — never «damage» or «abuse».
  • Every AI Coach answer cites a source — AASLD / AGA / EASL / Mayo Clinic. No citation = refusal pivot.
  • No diagnosis language. «Your ALT 85 maps to the elevated range per AASLD», never «you have NASH».
  • Plain-English labs. Every ALT / AST / GGT / FIB-4 value gets a verdict pill plus a one-line explanation a non-clinician understands.
  • Liver Score is a trend, not a verdict. Always show the 6-month direction — ALT lags weight loss by months, so a single absolute number demotivates.
  • Privacy-first. Labs and food photos stay on-device by default; iCloud sync is opt-in. RAG runs on-device (sqlite-vec).

3.2 · Feature consilium — 4 rounds

Round 1 · cohort demand

All 6 cohorts score each feature 0–5

Each cohort scored every feature on «how much do I need this», audience-weighted (Nick .25 · Greta .15 · Pete .20 · Rachel .08 · Patricia .15 · Robert .17). Pure demand ranks Lab Interpreter (4.50), Multi-Lab Dashboard (4.47) and AI Coach (4.25) at the top.

But demand alone buries two category-defining features: Alcohol Tracker (2.96) and FibroScan (2.41) score low because each serves a single anchor cohort — yet they are the highest-conversion features for Robert and Rachel and they are pure category whitespace. Round 1 demand alone would mis-build the roadmap → re-vote.

Round 2 · strategic re-vote + revision

Founder + ASO + engineering re-score Conversion · Moat · Build

Every feature re-scored 0–10 on Conversion (drives trial→paid), Moat (category whitespace / differentiation) and Build (10 = fastest, most sibling reuse). Revisions logged this round:

  • Alcohol Tracker → moat 10. The GGT-recovery timeline is genuinely uncontested — no liver app serves the sober community (r/stopdrinking 500K).
  • FibroScan → moat 9. Rachel's $40K-drug cohort has zero dedicated tracking tools today.
  • Recipe Engine → moat cut to 4. MyFitnessPal and Mediterranean recipe apps crowd this lane heavily.
  • PCOS Cross-Link → build 4. Gated on the Cysta AI integration handshake — not a self-contained build.
