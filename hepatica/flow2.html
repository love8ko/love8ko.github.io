Today answers "how am I right now"; Progress answers "how far have I come". Log — the «+» FAB, the highest-frequency interaction

Where the loop changed the design

Three places re-evaluation overruled the first build

  • Coach v1 → v2. v1 took "panel of specialists" literally — three lenses, every answer. The user-POV pass caught the overload; v2 collapsed it to a verdict-first answer with the panel one tap away. The consilium idea survived; the literal execution didn't.
  • The tab order. The locked bar was Today · Labs · +Log · Coach · Progress. Re-evaluated against frequency: Coach is a near-daily surface, Labs is quarterly. The bar was re-ordered to Today · Coach · +Log · Progress · Labs — Coach promoted, Labs moved to the calm edge.
  • Day-1 empty state. First built only for Robert. Re-evaluated as a churn risk that hits every cohort — re-worked into six designed skeletons (§8.6), each with its own ghost chart and step-zero CTA.
