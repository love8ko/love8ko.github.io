Hepatica 🌿

Reverse fatty liver. Reclaim your vitality.

AI Fatty Liver Companion — for NAFLD / MASLD and liver recovery

Hepatica is an educational companion for adults living with, or worried about, fatty liver disease (NAFLD / MASLD), elevated liver enzymes, and alcohol-associated liver stress. Whether you were just diagnosed, are on Ozempic, are managing pre-diabetes, are taking Rezdiffra, or are recovering from alcohol — Hepatica gives you one place to understand, track, and improve your liver health.
