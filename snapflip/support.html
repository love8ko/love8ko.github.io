Frequently Asked Questions

How does SnapFlip work?

Point your camera at an item, choose a photo, or type its name. SnapFlip's AI identifies it and estimates its resale price in your currency, with current eBay listings from your country to compare. Enter what the shelf asks to see your profit after estimated fees and shipping, a Flip Score (1–100) and WORTH IT, MAYBE or SKIP. Tap Create listing for a draft formatted for eBay, Vinted, Depop, Mercari or Poshmark.

Does SnapFlip offer a free trial?

Eligible new subscribers who choose Weekly Access receive a 7-day free trial. Pro access starts immediately. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, Weekly Access automatically converts to the paid weekly plan at the price shown by Apple. Annual Access has no introductory trial and is billed immediately.

What is the Flip Score?

The Flip Score is a 1–100 rating of how good the flip looks: it grows with your estimated profit margin after fees and shipping, and stays lower when the price estimate is rough. 70 and up is a great flip; 30 and below usually means skip it.

How accurate are the prices?

The price comes from AI and, where your country has an eBay marketplace, the asking prices of current eBay listings for the same item. Asking prices are not sold prices, so every price is an estimate, not an appraisal. Answer the quick questions or add a photo of the label to sharpen it, and check the listings and the item before you buy or sell.

What items can I scan?

SnapFlip works with virtually any resellable item: clothing, sneakers, electronics, books, toys, home decor, accessories, collectibles, vintage items, and more. For best results, take a clear, well-lit photo of a single item.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel through your iPhone: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → SnapFlip → Cancel Subscription. To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current billing period ends. Your access continues until the end of the current paid period. Billing and refund requests are handled by Apple.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you reinstalled the app or switched devices, go to Settings (tab) → Restore Purchases. This will restore your active subscription. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

The scan didn't identify my item correctly. What should I do?

AI identification works best with clear, well-lit photos of single items. Try these tips: take the photo in good lighting, avoid blurry images, show any brand labels or logos, and photograph the item from the front. If the AI still misidentifies the item, it may be a very unique or uncommon item that falls outside the AI's training data.

Does SnapFlip work offline?

New scans, prices and listing drafts need an internet connection. Everything saved in My Flips stays on your iPhone and opens offline.

Is my data private?

My Flips stays on your iPhone. Photos and the details you type go to our AI providers to identify and price items; only search words go to eBay to show listings. SnapFlip 2.0 uses RevenueCat for subscriptions and has no ad tracking. You choose what to allow on the consent screen and can change it in Settings. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What is the listing generator?

On a scanned item, tap "Create listing" for a title, description and price. Switch between eBay, Vinted, Depop, Mercari and Poshmark to reformat it for that marketplace, then copy or share it and post it yourself. SnapFlip is independent and not affiliated with these marketplaces and never posts for you.
