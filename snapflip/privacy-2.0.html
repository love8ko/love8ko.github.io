SnapFlip 2.0 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

SnapFlip is developed by Valeriy Loveiko. This page describes SnapFlip 2.0, including its TestFlight builds. Earlier versions follow the previous-version policy. SnapFlip helps you identify items, compare marketplace examples, prepare listings and record your own flips. AI results and market samples are estimates you must verify; they do not guarantee authenticity, condition, a sale or profit.

What is processed and why

DataPurpose and service providers
Photos you capture or select, item hintsSent over HTTPS through our Cloudflare Worker to OpenAI for item identification. We do not send your RevenueCat identifier to OpenAI with the photo.
Item names, checked notes and separately labelled unverified photo suggestionsSent through Cloudflare to DeepSeek to prepare listing text. Item photos are not sent to DeepSeek. A limited selection of marketplace titles and model checkpoints may also be used to select inspection guidance for shared collections. Do not include personal or sensitive information in item notes.
Search terms, chosen marketplace, country and item-price limitProcessed by Cloudflare and the selected source: eBay Sold Listings API for eBay listings and sold references; Extractr for Vinted listings; Swanum for Vinted sold observations. These are independent services. A sold observation may be a last visible asking price rather than the final transaction amount.
Subscription and technical access dataApple handles purchases. RevenueCat processes its anonymous app user identifier, product and subscription status. Our server checks SnapFlip access, stores a short-lived access cache and enforces usage limits. Cloudflare processes network information needed to deliver and protect requests; quota identifiers derived from IP addresses are hashed.
Consent and product eventsCloudflare D1 stores consent version, date and each choice, plus limited product event names and dates linked to a pseudonymous app identifier. Events exclude photos, listing drafts, transaction amounts and free-form notes.

SnapFlip 2.0 does not include AppsFlyer attribution, does not request advertising tracking permission, and does not display third-party advertisements or sell personal information.

Local records and retention

  • Your My Flips photos, item records, drafts, amounts, dates and local goals are saved on this iPhone. Saved checks may be linked from earlier scan data. Individual records can be deleted in the app. Export important records before removing the app; SnapFlip does not provide a cloud backup of My Flips. Device backups are controlled by your Apple settings.
  • Our Worker does not save photo or listing-draft request bodies. OpenAI's standard API abuse-monitoring logs may be retained for up to 30 days, subject to its policy and exceptions.
  • DeepSeek processes text in China. We do not promise a fixed retention period for DeepSeek's provider logs; its applicable terms and policies govern that processing. Deleting a local draft does not itself delete provider logs.
  • Shared marketplace snapshots are cached for approximately one day and contain marketplace observations rather than your private My Flips records. Saved cloud searches and matches remain until you delete them or until 30 days after your access ends. Monitoring stops when access ends. Shared collections refresh at most daily while in use; arbitrary saved models are checked manually.
  • Product events are kept for up to 90 days and are also removed with the expired cloud-search cleanup. Consent records remain to document permissions after that automatic cleanup; Settings → Delete cloud data removes your server-side consent and cloud records as well. Temporary quota/access records expire through scheduled cleanup.

Your choices

Before the first AI or marketplace request, SnapFlip asks for one versioned consent with separate choices. The “What's shared” links explain providers before you agree. A change of provider or purpose requires updated consent. Settings lets you withdraw AI permission, stop and remove cloud searches, delete cloud data, and export local records. Withdrawal stops new requests in that scope; it does not erase completed local work or automatically erase third-party logs. Deleting cloud data does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel subscriptions in iPhone Settings → your Apple account → Subscriptions.

When a subscription ends, new paid checks and AI work stop. Existing My Flips records remain available for reading, exporting, deleting and recording a sale or costs for an already started flip. Paused cloud candidates are hidden. Restoring access before the cleanup period permits checking again; it does not guarantee that a marketplace listing is still available.

If you believe a child supplied personal information, contact us. We update this page when data practices change and request fresh in-app consent when needed.
