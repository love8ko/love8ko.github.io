Two Sparrows — Support

A Bible verse for how you feel, on your Lock Screen.

Write to us any time: sergejkar@gmail.com. We usually answer within two days.

How do I put the verse on my Lock Screen?

  1. Touch and hold your Lock Screen.
  2. Tap Customize, then choose Lock Screen.
  3. Tap the widget row under the clock.
  4. Choose Two Sparrows — the verse widget.

In the App, Widgets → "Show me" plays a short guide, and "Check my widget" confirms it's there.

How do I add it to my Home Screen or StandBy?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → search "Two Sparrows" → pick a size. For StandBy, add the widget while your iPhone is charging on its side.

How does the verse change?

In the morning you get a new verse for the day. When you choose how you feel, the widget follows that feeling for the day. After 8 PM it turns to a verse for the night in warm candle colours. "Keep it on my Lock Screen" pins a verse until the next morning.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Two Sparrows. Cancelling during the 7-day trial means you are not charged. We send a reminder before the trial ends if notifications are on.

I bought on another iPhone

Open the App → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Audio doesn't play

Recordings download the first time you listen and then work offline. Check your connection and try again.

Where does the Scripture come from?

Berean Standard Bible (public domain); prayers from the Book of Common Prayer (public domain); topical lists from OpenBible.info (CC BY 4.0). Scripture only — no ads.
