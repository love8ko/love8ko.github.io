Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Two Sparrows: Bible Widget ("Two Sparrows" or "the App") is developed and published by Valerii Loveiko. This policy explains what the App keeps on your device, what it sends to outside services, and the choices you have.

Summary: No account is required. Your choices, saved verses and settings stay on your device. When you type or say what's on your heart, that text is sent through our server only to pick fitting Bible verses and is not stored. Purchases are handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We show no ads, we do not sell data, and we use no analytics or tracking SDKs.

1. Data stored on your device

  • What you chose in onboarding (for example "Anxious"), your rhythm preferences (morning, through the day, night) and the evening prayer time
  • Verses you save with ♥, the verse you keep on your Lock Screen, text size and appearance
  • The state your widgets use, shared between the App and its widgets on your device
  • Audio recordings of verses and prayers downloaded once and kept in the App's cache

This data is not sent to us and the developer cannot access it. Device backups may include it, depending on your Apple settings. Deleting the App deletes it.

2. Data sent to outside services

2.1 Finding a verse in your own words

If you use "Tell me in your own words" (typing or speaking), the text (up to 500 characters) is sent to our Cloudflare Worker, which asks DeepSeek to choose fitting verse references from the App's Bible library. The model only picks references; it never writes Scripture or advice. Our server does not store or log your text (it records only the text length and the chosen state for abuse protection). DeepSeek processes the text to return the answer under its own terms. If the service is unavailable, the App picks verses on your device instead. Before anything is sent, the App checks your words on your device, and if they suggest you may be in danger it shows the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

Speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition, on your device when your iPhone supports it; otherwise Apple processes it under Apple's privacy policy. No recording is kept.

2.2 Audio

When you tap Listen, the App downloads the recording of that verse or prayer from our server (Cloudflare). The request contains the verse or prayer identifier, the App version and an anonymous purchase identifier. Recordings are made from the public-domain Bible and prayer texts with a synthetic voice (Google Gemini); no personal data is sent to Google.

2.3 Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. The App uses RevenueCat to check your subscription status with an anonymous identifier created by RevenueCat; it receives purchase information (product, dates, status), not your name or payment details. Apple and RevenueCat process this data under their own privacy policies.

2.4 Our server

Cloudflare, which runs our server, processes your IP address to deliver requests and to protect the service from abuse (rate limits). We do not use IP addresses to identify you.

3. Notifications

If you allow them, evening prayer notifications are scheduled on your device. No push server is used.

4. What we do not do

  • No ads, no advertising or analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No account, no email collection

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Your choices

  • You can use the App without typing or speaking anything: choose a state instead
  • You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings
  • You can delete all App data by deleting the App
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions

7. Changes and contact

We will update this page if the App's data practices change and change the effective date above. Questions: sergejkar@gmail.com.
