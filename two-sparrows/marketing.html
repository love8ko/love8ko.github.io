What's inside

A living Lock Screen verseA new verse every morning, a calm verse for the night after 8 PM, and the full verse sized to fit — never cut off.
Verses for how you feelAnxious, afraid, grieving, lonely, can't sleep — or tell it in your own words, by text or voice, and get verses chosen from the Bible.
Listen and prayVerses and prayers read aloud, and an evening prayer at the time you choose.
7-day pathsA verse, a prayer and one small step a day — for peace, fear, grief, loneliness, sleep, mornings, the Psalms and the words of Jesus. Miss a day and nothing resets.
Read in contextOpen the whole chapter around any verse, with short notes on who is speaking and why it matters.
Home Screen & StandBySmall, medium and large widgets in Linen, Paper, Candle and dark looks, with large text if you want it.
Share a verseMake a clean picture of a verse — Story, Square or Wallpaper — and send it to someone who needs it.
Keep what speaks to youSave verses with ♥ and keep one on your Lock Screen until morning.

Scripture only

Every verse is the Berean Standard Bible, word for word. Every prayer comes from the Book of Common Prayer. Two Sparrows never writes Scripture: when you describe what's on your heart, it only chooses verses from the Bible. No ads, no social feed, one notification a day at most — and only if you ask for it.

Private by design

No account and no sign-up. Your feelings, saved verses and settings stay on your iPhone. If you choose to use your own words, the text is used only to pick verses and is not stored by us. No ads, no tracking. Read the Privacy Policy.

Two Sparrows offers Scripture and prayer for encouragement; it is not crisis or medical care. If you are in danger or thinking about suicide, call or text 988 (US) or your local emergency number.
