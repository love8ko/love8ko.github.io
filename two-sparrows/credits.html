Berean Standard Bible (BSB) — the translation used throughout the App.

Prayers

The Book of Common Prayer

1662 (Church of England), from the transcription by Lynda M. Howell of the standard text. Traditional-language (Rite One) prayers from the 1979 Book of Common Prayer of the Episcopal Church (USA), most carried over from the 1928 American book, as published at bcponline.org. Public domain. In the United Kingdom the rights in the 1662 Book of Common Prayer are vested in the Crown.

Topical and cross-reference data from OpenBible.info (openbible.info), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0). Used to build the library of verses for each feeling; selections reviewed and adapted by the Two Sparrows editors.
