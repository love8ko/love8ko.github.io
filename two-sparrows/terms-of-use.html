2. Content

Scripture comes from the Berean Standard Bible (public domain). Prayers come from the Book of Common Prayer (1662, and traditional-language prayers from the 1979 US edition, public domain). Topical verse lists and cross-references are based on OpenBible.info (CC BY 4.0). Recordings are read by a synthetic voice from these public-domain texts. Notes on verses and the small steps in paths are written by the Two Sparrows editors. Full credits: Licences & credits.

The App never writes Scripture or prayers. When you describe what's on your heart, an automated service chooses verse references from the App's library; it can choose imperfectly, and you can always pick a feeling or another verse instead.
