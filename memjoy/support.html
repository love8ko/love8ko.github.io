Memjoy: Brain Games for Adults · Loveiko Labs

Memjoy is a daily practice for memory and attention. A friendly voice host talks with you, makes short scenes from your own interests, and offers games on touch. It is entertainment and a way to keep your mind active. It is not a medical product.

Write to us

A question, an idea, or something broke: write to loveykovl@gmail.com. A real person reads it and answers. In the app you can do the same from Settings → Write to us.

How do I answer?

Whichever is easier for you. You can answer out loud, or tap the large buttons on the screen. The host does not hurry you: take your time. Where the app offers it, you can also type.

The microphone and speech permission

To hear your spoken answers, Memjoy asks iPhone for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. If you said No, or voice does not work, you can still play with the buttons. To change your mind:

  1. Open iPhone Settings and scroll down to Memjoy.
  2. Turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition.

Then return to Memjoy.

Voice and cloud features: how to turn them off

Before the app uses the cloud, it shows a consent screen with three choices (in the current pilot the text is in Russian): that you are 18 or older and understand these are games, not medical help ("Мне есть 18 лет; это игры, не медицинская помощь"); that an AI talks to you by voice ("ИИ общается со мной голосом"); and that AI prepares games from your answers ("ИИ готовит игры по моим ответам"). Each has a "Details" link ("Подробнее") before you agree.

To withdraw: open Settings → Privacy and turn off Cloud features. After that the host plays ready-made scenes, and the scene programme, scene archive and scene audio on your iPhone are erased. Personal details from your introduction are deleted from our server. "My world" stays on your iPhone. To remove it too, see below.

Delete my data

Open Settings → Your data:

  • Delete album stories removes the stories in your Album.
  • Delete everything: album, name, progress removes the album, your name and all progress, and the programme starts over. Deleting is final.

Cloud copy. If you turned on the optional cloud copy, open Settings → "My world between devices" (Мой мир между устройствами) and tap "Disconnect and delete cloud copy" (Отключить и удалить облачную копию). "Delete everything" also deletes the cloud copy. Entries on your iPhone stay until you delete them. More in the Privacy Policy.

Sign in with Apple

It is optional. Without it everything stays on your iPhone. You only sign in if you choose to keep your "My world" on other devices or after changing phones. We receive an account identifier, not your name or email. The cloud copy has no recording of your voice.

Subscriptions, refunds, a gift for Mom

Memjoy has a weekly plan with a free trial for new subscribers and an annual plan. The app shows the price and any trial before you pay. In the app, Settings → Subscription shows whether your plan is active.

  • Cancel: iPhone Settings → your name (Apple ID) → Subscriptions → Memjoy → Cancel Subscription. Do it at least 24 hours before the period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel.
  • Restore on a new phone: sign in with the same Apple ID you bought with, then tap Restore on the subscription screen or Restore purchases in Settings (Восстановить покупки).
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Family Sharing: where enabled, you can buy it once and share it with a parent through Apple Family Sharing.

Is this a dementia test?

No. Memjoy does not test, diagnose or screen for dementia or any illness, and it does not tell you how your memory compares with other people. It is a pleasant daily practice.

If you are worried about your memory, please talk to a doctor.

Large text

Memjoy uses big buttons and follows your iPhone text size. To make text larger: iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text. You can also choose a Day or Night look in Memjoy: Settings → Appearance.
