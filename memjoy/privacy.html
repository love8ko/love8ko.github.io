Privacy Policy

In short.

  • Memjoy plays short word-and-attention games with an AI host. It is entertainment for adults 18 and older. It is not medical care.
  • If you turn on cloud features, a clip of your voice answer goes through our server to Google Gemini, which turns it into text; text of what you said or typed also goes to Google to find topics and build your scene.   • We do not make a voiceprint and do not identify you by your voice. No ads, no tracking, no sale of your data, and we do not train our own AI models on it.
  • This version of Memjoy has no purchases. If that changes, Apple will process payment and we will not see your card.

1. Who we are

Memjoy: Brain Games for Adults ("Memjoy", "the app") is an iOS app made by Loveiko Labs ("we", "us"), a small independent app developer. You can reach us at loveykovl@gmail.com. The legal entity and postal address will be added here (see the notes at the end).

This policy explains what the app collects, why, which companies help us process it, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it.

2. What we collect and why

We collect only what is needed to run the games. We do not ask for your email address or phone number. You can use Memjoy without cloud features; then speech recognition happens only on your iPhone and the host plays ready-made scenes.

Your voice answers

When you answer aloud, the app records a short clip of that answer. With cloud features on, the clip is sent through our server to Google Gemini, which writes down what you said. The same clip is also recognized on your iPhone, and the app uses whichever result arrives in time. With cloud features off, only the on-iPhone recognition is used. See section 5.

What you say or type about yourself

You can tell the host about yourself by voice or by typing. With cloud features on, the text goes through our server to Google Gemini, which picks out topics, objects and short quotes from your own words. You see them as cards and choose what to keep. In the cards, close family appear only as a role such as "daughter", without names. Instructions to the AI and automatic filters keep health, politics, money, crime, death, religion and intimate life out of the cards, but this is automated and can miss. Confirmed topics are saved in "My world" on your iPhone. To build a scene, the app sends Google up to three topic titles with short quotes (up to 200 characters each).

The host's voice

Our server writes the host's lines and sends them as text to ElevenLabs, which returns audio. These lines can include words, names of things and places, and titles from your topics. ElevenLabs does not receive a recording of your voice.

Your name (optional)

If you add a name in Settings, it is stored on your iPhone and is not part of the cloud copy. The current visit format does not send it to our servers. A retired older show format that is still inside the app can include it in the text sent to Google and ElevenLabs so the host can greet you by name.

Install ID

The app creates a random install ID. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. It is sent with every request to our server and used to count daily requests, so we can keep costs and abuse under control. It is stored in the iPhone's Keychain, which can survive deleting the app, so reinstalling may reuse the same ID. The app has no button to reset it.

Your consent record

When you agree on the consent screen, the app saves on your iPhone which version of the text you agreed to, when, and each box. If you turn on the optional cloud copy, our server also stores which version of its consent you accepted.

Optional cloud copy

If you switch it on, your confirmed topics, album pages and written stories are stored on our server. See section 7.

Usage counts and service logs

Our server counts requests per install ID per day (see section 6). Its logs record only things like the route, a result code and sizes. They contain no recordings, no text of what you said, and no IDs. Cloudflare, which hosts the server, necessarily sees the IP address of each request and keeps its own network logs under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Test builds

Builds distributed through TestFlight to testers also send technical diagnostics (event names and timings, no recordings) that are kept for 24 hours, and can offer an experimental live voice conversation in which your speech goes through our server to ElevenLabs and the conversation text is deleted from our server within 24 hours after the session ends (ElevenLabs keeps data under its own policy). These are not part of the App Store version.

3. What stays on your iPhone

  • Your "My world" topics with the quotes you confirmed, album pages, written stories, results, the log of recognized answers, your name and your settings are stored in the app's storage on your iPhone.
  • The app's data is not excluded from iPhone backups, so it can be included in your iCloud or computer backups of the iPhone. The app itself does not sync it to iCloud.
  • Recordings of your voice are not saved. The clip of an answer exists only in memory while it is sent, plus a short temporary file for on-iPhone recognition that is deleted right after.
  • The host's audio is saved on your iPhone: a cache that iOS may clear, and, for scenes you played and for album pages, files kept until you delete them.
  • Daily reminders, if you turn them on, are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. We do not send push notifications.
  • If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it, except what is kept in your iPhone backups, the install ID in Keychain, and anything in the optional cloud copy (delete that first, see section 8).

5. Voice data notice

A recording of your voice may count as biometric or voice data under laws such as the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA), the Texas Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act (CUBI) and Washington's biometric law (RCW 19.375). We take that into account: we never create a voiceprint, and we follow the retention and destruction rules below.

  • We do not create a voiceprint and do not use your voice to identify or recognize you. The app turns what you said into text and nothing more.
  • Purpose. Only to understand your spoken answers and stories so the game can respond. Not for any other use.
  • Consent. Before any clip leaves your iPhone, the consent screen asks you to tick boxes, including one for the AI host talking with you by voice, and offers a "Details" sheet that names the providers. Without it, the app uses only on-iPhone recognition.
  • Who receives it. Our server (Cloudflare), which passes the clip on, and Google, to write it down.
  • Retention and destruction. On your iPhone the clip is held in memory while the answer is recorded and sent, and in a short temporary file for on-iPhone recognition that is deleted right after; it is never saved. Our server does not keep the clip after the request finishes. Google keeps it only for a limited period under the Gemini API terms (section 4).
  • No sale. We do not sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from voice data.
  • No other disclosure, unless you ask us to, or the law or a court order requires it.
  • Protection. Sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Provider keys are kept on our server, not in the app.

This notice is our public retention and destruction policy for voice data.

7. Optional cloud copy and Sign in with Apple

By default your world stays on your iPhone. If you want it on another device or after changing phones, you can switch on the cloud copy. It is a separate choice from cloud features: you tick its own box, read its own "Details", and sign in with Apple.

  • What is stored: topics you confirmed with your quotes, close family by role only, album pages (title, topic, objects and captions of the scene, what you did on the page), and stories you wrote. No recordings, no transcripts of conversations, no scores, no diagnostics, no host audio, and not your name from Settings.
  • Sign in with Apple: the app asks Apple for no name and no email. We receive an identifier for your Apple account, and we store only a one-way hash of it.
  • Where: a database on Cloudflare, created in North America.
  • How long: until you delete it. "Forget" on a topic removes the topic and the pages linked to it. "Disconnect and delete cloud copy" removes the whole account on our server. Database backups may keep deleted data for up to 30 days.
  • If you are offline when you delete, the app remembers the request and repeats it. No new data is sent in the meantime.

8. Your choices, consent and deleting data

The app is currently in Russian. The exact labels are given in English with the Russian label in brackets.

  • Withdraw consent to cloud features: Settings → Privacy → "Cloud features" (Настройки → «Конфиденциальность» → «Облачные функции»), switch off. After that the app sends no new answers to our server, plays ready-made scenes, uses only on-iPhone recognition, deletes the scene schedule and saved host audio from your iPhone, and asks our server to delete any test-session data. What is saved in "My world" and your album stays on your iPhone. This switch does not remove the optional cloud copy: use the next item.
  • Delete the cloud copy: Settings → "My world across devices" (Мой мир между устройствами) → "Disconnect and delete cloud copy" (Отключить и удалить облачную копию). Your data on the iPhone stays.
  • Forget a topic: in "My world", "Forget this" (Забыть это) removes the topic, its pages, and, if the cloud copy is on, the matching records on our server.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Your data → "Delete everything: album, name, progress" (Ваши данные → «Удалить всё: альбом, имя, прогресс»). This clears the data in the app on your iPhone, deletes the cloud copy if you have it, and asks our server to delete test-session data. "Delete album stories" (Удалить истории из Альбома) removes only the written stories.
  • What these do not remove: the install ID in your Keychain and the daily counters on our server (about 1–2 days), which are not personal content; the host-audio cache on our server (up to 30 days); data at Google and ElevenLabs; and your own iPhone backups.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: you can turn them off in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Reminders: turn them off in Memjoy Settings or iOS Settings.

To ask us anything about your data, see section 18.

9. What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.
  • No tracking across other apps and websites.
  • No sale of personal information, and no sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • No voiceprint, and no identifying you by your voice.
  • No training of our own AI models on your data.

10. Subscriptions and Apple purchases

This version of Memjoy has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. If we add them, they will be sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple will process the payment; we will not receive your card details, your name or your Apple ID. We will update this policy, and tell you in the app, before we begin using any service to check or record purchases. You can manage or cancel a subscription in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

11. Not medical care

Memjoy is a set of entertaining word-and-attention games. It does not diagnose, treat or screen for anything, and it does not promise to improve memory or protect against any condition. The AI host can make mistakes. If your memory worries you, talk to a doctor.

If you are in crisis or thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 (US), or your local emergency number.

12. Children

Memjoy is only for people 18 and older. The consent screen asks you to confirm you are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 used the app, contact us and we will delete what we hold.

15. Where data is processed

Our server runs on Cloudflare's global network, and the optional cloud copy database was created in North America. Google and ElevenLabs process data in their own facilities, which may be in the United States or other countries. By using cloud features you understand that your data may be processed outside your state or country. For people in the EU or UK, we are still confirming the legal mechanism for these transfers with counsel (see the notes at the end of this policy).

16. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Provider keys live only on our server, never in the app. Your cloud sign-in session and Apple account identifier are kept in the iPhone's Keychain, and on our server only as one-way hashes. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee it.
