Privacy Policy — WatchSnap

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Overview

WatchSnap is made by Loveiko Labs. It helps you identify watches, check them for red flags and see asking prices. This page says what leaves your phone, who receives it and why. The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name or email.

On your device

Your scans, photos, saved watches, watches you follow in Value, notes and reminders are stored on your device.

Your iCloud copy

If iCloud is on, the app also keeps a copy of your saved watches, their photos and your price checks in your own private iCloud account (Apple CloudKit), so you can bring them back on a new iPhone. That copy belongs to your Apple account and we cannot read it. We do not keep a copy. You can stop the copy or delete it in Settings under Your data (“Delete my iCloud copy”).

Photos, lookups and chat

These happen only when you tap the matching button, and only after you agree on the first screen.

  • Scan. The photo or photos you take go through our server on Cloudflare to OpenAI (USA), which identifies the watch.
  • Red-flag check. When you tap Check, up to five photos (the main photo and close-ups of the dial, the inner ring or serial number, the caseback and the clasp), together with the brand, model and reference of that watch, go through our server to OpenAI (USA).
  • Finding a model by name. The name you type goes through our server to OpenAI (USA). The answer is kept on our server for 30 days without any ID and is shown to other people who type the same name. When you choose a model for your watch, we count the choice with a one-way code made from your subscription, so a model chosen by two subscribers can be marked as checked. We keep that code and the model entry until the entry is removed.
  • Chat. Your messages, with the details of the watch you are asking about (brand, model, reference, your notes, the scan result) and its current asking prices, go through our server to DeepSeek (China). Photos are not sent to chat.
  • Listing draft. The details of the watch you choose go through our server to DeepSeek (China). No photos are sent.

Our server does not save your photos or messages, and our logs do not contain them. The AI providers may keep what they receive for a period set by their own terms.

Phones set to mainland China

If your phone is set to Simplified Chinese with China (mainland) as its region, the app uses a server we run on Alibaba Cloud in Hangzhou, China, instead of the Cloudflare route. Your requests go straight to that server, which sees your network address. There, scans, red-flag checks and name lookups are processed by Alibaba Cloud (Qwen model), and chat and listing drafts by DeepSeek. To check your subscription and limits, to use the shared model list and to get prices, that server calls our other services described on this page. In this case your photos and messages are not sent to OpenAI.

Asking prices

When you tap to check a price, the watch reference, your country (taken from your phone’s region setting) and an anonymous app ID are sent to our price service, WatchMarket. If you use the deal check, the price you typed is sent too. No photos and no names are sent.

WatchMarket reads public marketplace listings through a web data provider, context.dev (USA), and through the eBay Browse API. From eBay we keep a listing’s title, price and condition, and only the seller’s country and whether the seller is a business, for at most 24 hours; the app shows eBay data only when it is less than 6 hours old. No seller names, links or photos are kept. Results are cached for 24 hours and shared across users.

Our server passes your network address to WatchMarket, which keeps only a one-way hash of it.

The weekly list of new watches

Loading this list reaches our server on Cloudflare (or the Alibaba Cloud server in mainland China), which sees your network address. No account identifier is sent with this request. Names, references, dates and retail prices in the list are as reported by the publications named on each watch. When you open an article, you go to that publication’s website. It sees your network address, may use its own cookies, and follows its own privacy policy. We do not control those sites.

Usage limits

To prevent abuse, our server counts scans, red-flag checks, name lookups, chat messages and price checks against an anonymous app ID, your subscription’s identifier and a one-way hash of your network address. It also remembers your subscription status for up to 7 days. These records are deleted automatically within about a week. WatchMarket keeps daily counts per anonymous app ID and hourly counts per network-address hash for its own limits; they hold no names. They are deleted automatically: the daily counts after 35 days and the hourly counts after 8 days.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. RevenueCat (USA) records your purchases and subscription status against an anonymous app ID, and receives your network address when the app talks to it. It starts when you open the app. Our server asks RevenueCat whether your subscription is active, using that ID. We do not receive your payment details.

Analytics and advertising

WatchSnap does not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs or tracking.

Your choices

You can withdraw your consent in Settings. Scans, red-flag checks, name lookups, chat, listing drafts, price checks and the weekly list then stop, and your saved watches stay on your device. You can stop the iCloud copy or delete it in Settings under Your data. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device but not the iCloud copy, so delete that copy first if you want it gone.

Deleting your data

Because there are no accounts, most of your data is on your device or in your iCloud, where you control it. To delete or correct anything held by our services, or to ask about it, write to us at the address below and include the install ID shown at the bottom of the email that opens from Settings → Write to us. We will remove what we can link to that ID. Data held by an AI provider or another provider named on this page follows that provider’s own rules.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. We process your data with your consent and to provide the app you subscribed to. For anything we or our providers hold, contact us below.

International transfers

The providers named above process data in the United States and, for chat, listing drafts and phones set to mainland China, in China. These countries may not offer the same protections as your own.

Children

WatchSnap is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date. If the change affects what you agreed to, the app will ask you again.
