Передача в production

Полная E-серия содержит восемь интерфейсных концептов. Выбор визуального направления и готовность функций — разные статусы. Приложение, API и симулятор не менялись.

E01 · Heartburn Keeping You Up?

Try an earlier dinner tomorrow night.

A late meal followed soon by lying down may matter. For a future evening, leaving at least 3 hours after eating before lying down may help.

E02 · What Can I Order With Reflux?

Ask for sauce on the side.

Citrus and rich sauces can bother some people. Your own response may differ.

E03 · Understand Your Test Results.

Inflammation of the food pipe lining.

Your report mentions esophagitis. Ask your clinician what this means for your care.

E04 · Make Tonight’s Plan Simple.

Finish dinner by 7:00 PM.

Your chosen bedtime is 10:00 PM. This plan leaves 3 hours after dinner before lying down; this interval may help nighttime symptoms.

E05 · Keep Your Treatment Notes Together.

Medication taken. Evening heartburn noted.

Your own entries, kept together. These notes do not establish how well a treatment works.

E06 · Throat or Voice Bothering You?

Hoarse voice. Throat clearing. Notes saved.

Record what you notice and when. Throat and voice symptoms can have different causes; these notes do not identify a diagnosis.

E07 · Meals & Symptoms. One Timeline.

Dinner 8:30 PM. Heartburn 10:15 PM.

Two saved events from the same evening. Their timing alone does not show that the meal caused the symptom.

E08 · Bring Your History to Your Appointment.

Your saved observations, ready to share.

A summary of your saved meals, symptoms, treatment notes and report. Share it as a starting point for your appointment.
