Why these two people

The audience hypothesis is a person whose recurring reflux interrupts sleep, food choices and everyday life. The project’s Brian and Frank portraits informed the life situations. The chosen ages and gender alternatives are creative directions, not measured customer demographics. Each lead keeps the same appearance across all three scenes using image references.

The two series share headlines, sublines, device composition, dish image and demonstration values. This is a visual comparison, not a controlled conversion experiment.

01 · Interrupted sleep

Heartburn Keeping You Up?
Get a clearer next step tonight.

A bed-edge posture, sleeping partner and bedside clock make the nighttime interruption recognizable. A shared dinner → 3 hours → bed diagram makes the suggested interval visible in both the interface and enlarged result.

One enlarged result: Leave 3 hours before lying down.

After a meal, this interval may help with nighttime reflux symptoms.

02 · Before ordering

What Can I Eat With Reflux?
Check the menu before you order.

Both partners and the waiter are visible. A pointing hand and illustrated menu show that an order is being chosen. The plated salmon and separate sauce bowl are enlarged from the app image, not a served meal on the restaurant table.

One enlarged result: Ask for sauce on the side.

Citrus and rich sauces can bother some people. Your own response may differ.

03 · Just after the appointment

Reflux Tests. What Do They Mean?
Your report, in plain English.

A physician hands over a report in a recognizable clinic. The patient looks at the phone. A synthetic acid exposure time of 7.2% is placed on the paper and repeated in the interface. The phone is identified as Refluxora and an AI explanation, not a live doctor consultation.

One enlarged result: Time with acid in your esophagus.

AET measures the percentage of time at pH below 4. Your clinician interprets it in context.

Medical basis · NIDDK

Sources and production

Six new scene photographs were created with built-in ImageGen, plus one shared transparent food asset and its prompt. The tool does not expose a selectable model version. No claim is made that Imagen 2.5 was used.

Typography, the product interface, meal-to-bedtime diagram, AET value and explanation are deterministic. The owner-requested GERD • ACID REFLUX line appears below the brand. Headlines are approximately 168–170 px; sublines are 96–99 px in the 1320 × 2868 exports. Full export and source details are in the manifest.

Patient research informed the underlying situations: symptom impact and seeking help and dietary and lifestyle changes. We did not conduct patient interviews. Published reflux-monitoring recommendations support the AET metric definition; the demonstration gives no diagnostic verdict.
