Refluxora 🔥

Calm your reflux. Reclaim your meals.

AI GERD & LPR Companion — for severe PPI users, pregnancy reflux, foodies, polypharmacy seniors, off-PPI seekers, and LPR voice professionals

Refluxora is an educational companion for adults living with, or worried about, gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), laryngopharyngeal reflux / silent reflux (LPR), pregnancy-related heartburn, polypharmacy-aware reflux management, and PPI taper journeys. Whether your PPI stopped working, you're 38 weeks pregnant and can't sleep, you refuse to give up Italian dinners, you're on 7 medications, you're tapering off omeprazole, or your voice keeps disappearing from silent reflux — Refluxora gives you one cohort-aware place to understand, track, and steer your reflux.

Privacy first

Your reflux symptoms, RSI scores, meal and menu photos, medication and protocol logs, and AI Coach chat history live on your device only. Photos are processed by on-device Apple Vision OCR first; sent to OpenAI's vision model only as a fallback when on-device confidence is low and after you accept per-feature consent — then not retained. Coach messages route through a Cloudflare Worker proxy to DeepSeek V3 with zero retention. We never collect your name, email, restaurant names, or location. No advertising. No cross-app tracking SDKs. See the full Privacy Policy.
