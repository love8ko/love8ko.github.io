Understand meals, symptoms and lab results you already have.

Reflux questions between appointments? Start with one observation, keep the useful information and see a next step. Refluxora provides educational information and tracking for GERD and LPR.

Start with your own information

Understand a meal
Describe a meal or choose a photo. Review identified ingredients and a general food consideration. A photograph cannot reveal every ingredient or determine your personal triggers.
Keep related observations
Record symptoms, meals and throat or voice symptom scores. Add how you felt after a meal and return to your own history.
Explain an existing lab result
Upload a result photo, review recognized values and confirm them. Get an educational explanation and questions for your clinician. Missing units and reference ranges are not guessed.
Ask the AI assistant
Discuss a question or a selected record. Medical explanations use relevant source material; everyday conversation can continue without clinical citations. AI may ask for context and can be incorrect.
Choose one next step
Keep an action you choose in Plan. Review your observations and prepare a report for an appointment. The app does not prescribe treatment or order tests.
See an example journey
Clearly marked examples show how a history can grow. Examples are separate from personal records and are not forecasts of improved health.

FAQ & Support

Privacy and regional AI

Observations and conversations are saved locally. Selected photos, text and record context are also sent for cloud AI processing after consent. A mainland-China Apple storefront or CN device region uses Qwen through our Alibaba Cloud service; other regions use OpenAI and DeepSeek through Cloudflare. UI language alone does not select the server. A failed China request is not redirected to the global AI service. No app account is required. Billing is handled by Apple and RevenueCat. See our Privacy Policy for processing and retention.

Educational information

Refluxora does not diagnose, prescribe treatment or replace a qualified healthcare professional. AI can be incomplete or incorrect. Discuss medical decisions with your clinician. See our Medical Disclaimer.
