AI Providers & Clinical Sources

Updated 2026-09-12

Refluxora is grounded in published clinical guidelines but is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of the organisations, authors, or pharmaceutical manufacturers cited below. This page describes the AI providers that power Coach and Photo Scan, the clinical corpus those answers are grounded in, and our privacy promise around how your data is handled.

1. AI Providers

Refluxora uses Qwen through our dedicated Alibaba Cloud service in Beijing for mainland China, selected by Apple storefront or device region. Other regions use OpenAI and DeepSeek through our Cloudflare Worker. UI language does not select the provider. Mainland China requests are not redirected to the global service when an error occurs. No third-party AI keys ship inside the iOS app.

Qwen · mainland China

Alibaba Cloud Model Studio processes selected meal and lab photos, meal descriptions, questions, the current conversation and selected record context for the requested feature. Qwen also creates embeddings for source search. Search runs against a separate bundled corpus built with the same embedding model; original source identifiers and links are preserved. Our Alibaba Cloud Function Compute proxy does not log request or response content. Processing is through the mainland China workspace; Alibaba Cloud service terms apply.

OpenAI · other regions

  • Source retrieval — after explicit consent, your question and selected information are sent to OpenAI through our Worker to produce an embedding. The embedding searches the bundled source collection on your device. No separate account identifier is attached, but text may contain information you choose to include. Query embeddings are cached only during the app session.
  • Photo analysis and laboratory transcription — the selected photo is sent to OpenAI through our Worker. This is cloud analysis, not exclusively on-device OCR. A copy is saved locally with your record. Laboratory fields must be checked by you before saving and requesting an explanation.

Automatic observation explanations

This optional setting has its own consent in the app. After a saved observation, its title, rating or confirmed lab fields, date, meal details and linked previous observation are sent through the regional service to Qwen in mainland China, or to OpenAI for source-search embeddings and DeepSeek for explanation in other regions, together with retrieved excerpts. This request does not include photos, unrelated diary entries or chat history. Explanations are cached on the device. Turn the setting off under Settings → Privacy & Data → Automatic explanations; keeping records remains available.

DeepSeek · other regions

  • Coach explanations — your question, selected information, current conversation and retrieved excerpts are sent to DeepSeek through our Worker. Retrieval happens before answer generation. The app accepts source identifiers only from the excerpts supplied for that request. When evidence is insufficient, it asks for context or declines to draw a conclusion. Source matching does not guarantee medical accuracy.
  • Text meal analysis — your meal description is sent to DeepSeek for general ingredient considerations. This does not identify your personal triggers.

Cloudflare Worker (proxy)

  • Outside mainland China, OpenAI and DeepSeek are reached through Refluxora's Cloudflare Worker. Mainland China uses the dedicated Alibaba Cloud proxy described above.
  • The Worker does not log request or response payloads.
  • Cloudflare processes your connection to our service. The AI provider receives the proxy connection rather than a direct request from your device.
  • No user identifier (Apple ID, RevenueCat ID, email, IDFA/IDFV) is attached to AI calls.

2. Clinical Corpus

Coach searches a bundled source collection on your device using the embedding described above. The query text is sent externally to create that embedding; the current conversation and retrieved excerpts are sent for answer generation. Sources are shown with the result so you can inspect the material used. The collection includes clinical publications and patient-facing references; inclusion is not an endorsement or a guarantee of applicability. Examples include:

  • ACG 2022 — Clinical Guideline for the Diagnosis and Management of GERD (American College of Gastroenterology)
  • AGA 2024 — Best Practice Advice for Refractory GERD (American Gastroenterological Association)
  • AAO-HNS — LPR Position Paper (American Academy of Otolaryngology – Head and Neck Surgery)
  • Dr. Jamie Koufman + Dr. Jonathan Aviv — The Acid Watcher Diet and Acid Watcher Cookbook (low-acid protocols, vocal-fold hygiene, ENT-aligned reflux management)
  • Voquezna (vonoprazan) — FDA Prescribing Information (Phathom Pharmaceuticals label, U.S. only; omitted in markets without local regulatory approval)
  • ACOG — Pregnancy & Heartburn Clinical Guidance (American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists)
  • IFFGD — Lifestyle & Symptom-Management Reference (International Foundation for Gastrointestinal Disorders)
  • Mayo Clinic + Cleveland Clinic + AGS Beers Criteria — patient-facing references and elder-prescribing safety (American Geriatrics Society Beers Criteria for the Senior Sam cohort)

Source availability depends on the bundled collection and the question. Internal audience/persona documents are excluded from clinical retrieval. A matching source identifier does not establish that every statement is supported.

Refluxora cites these sources but is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of them.

3. Privacy Promise

  • Selected meal photos are uploaded when you choose Analyze. The photo is sent to Qwen through our Alibaba Cloud service in mainland China, or to OpenAI through Cloudflare in other regions. A local copy stays in your journal. AI results may be incomplete or incorrect.
  • AI requests can include the content you submit and relevant recent records. We do not attach your Apple ID or RevenueCat ID to AI requests. Content itself may contain information you choose to share.
  • RevenueCat handles billing. Product analytics, when enabled, uses a pseudonymous RevenueCat identifier to connect engagement and subscription events. Health records and AI content are excluded from those events.
  • Decline AI features anytime by declining a feature’s consent sheet. Automatic explanations can also be disabled separately in Settings → Privacy & Data. Deleting app data clears saved consents.
