AI Providers & Clinical Sources

Updated 2026-05-25

Refluxora is grounded in published clinical guidelines but is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of the organisations, authors, or pharmaceutical manufacturers cited below. This page describes the AI providers that power Coach and Photo Scan, the clinical corpus those answers are grounded in, and our privacy promise around how your data is handled.

1. AI Providers

Refluxora calls two large-language-model providers for two distinct features. Both calls are routed through a Cloudflare Worker proxy that we operate. No third-party AI keys ship inside the iOS app.

OpenAI

  • Embeddings — text-only RAG queries are embedded server-side to find the closest matching chunks in our on-device clinical corpus. Embedding inputs are anonymised; no user identifier is attached.
  • GPT-4o-mini Vision (fallback) — when on-device Apple Vision OCR returns a confidence score below 0.5 (handwritten menus, low-light photos, unusual fonts), Photo Scan falls back to GPT-4o-mini Vision to extract dish names. The original photo never leaves the device — only the OCR text request is sent.

DeepSeek V3

  • Coach RAG conversational responses — Coach answers are generated by DeepSeek V3 using the on-device clinical corpus chunks returned by the embedding step above. Every reply is cited per turn against the sources listed in §2.

Cloudflare Worker (proxy)

  • Both providers are reached only through Refluxora's Cloudflare Worker, which sits between your device and OpenAI / DeepSeek.
  • The Worker does not log request or response payloads.
  • Your outbound IP address is stripped at the Worker edge before the upstream call. We never see your IP.
  • No user identifier (Apple ID, RevenueCat ID, email, IDFA/IDFV) is attached to AI calls.

2. Clinical Corpus

Coach answers are grounded in a curated on-device sqlite-vec corpus. The corpus ships inside the app — RAG queries never leave the device as raw text against your full chat history. Sources cited:

  • ACG 2022 — Clinical Guideline for the Diagnosis and Management of GERD (American College of Gastroenterology)
  • AGA 2024 — Best Practice Advice for Refractory GERD (American Gastroenterological Association)
  • AAO-HNS — LPR Position Paper (American Academy of Otolaryngology – Head and Neck Surgery)
  • Dr. Jamie Koufman + Dr. Jonathan Aviv — The Acid Watcher Diet and Acid Watcher Cookbook (low-acid protocols, vocal-fold hygiene, ENT-aligned reflux management)
  • Voquezna (vonoprazan) — FDA Prescribing Information (Phathom Pharmaceuticals label, U.S. only; omitted in markets without local regulatory approval)
  • ACOG — Pregnancy & Heartburn Clinical Guidance (American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists)
  • IFFGD — Lifestyle & Symptom-Management Reference (International Foundation for Gastrointestinal Disorders)
  • Mayo Clinic + Cleveland Clinic + AGS Beers Criteria — patient-facing references and elder-prescribing safety (American Geriatrics Society Beers Criteria for the Senior Sam cohort)

Corpus version 2026-05 · 2,402 chunks · 64.8 MB on-device

Refluxora cites these sources but is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of them.

3. Privacy Promise

  • Camera + meal photos stay on-device. Only the AI's text verdict (good / watch / alert + reasoning) leaves the device. The original image is never uploaded.
  • RAG queries (text only) leave the device anonymised via the Cloudflare Worker proxy. We never see your IP, your Apple ID, or any user identifier.
  • RevenueCat handles billing. No name, email, or health data is tied to your subscription record.
  • Decline AI features anytime in Settings → Privacy → Reset AI consents. Once reset, the next Coach send or Photo Scan tap will re-present the first-use consent sheet, and AI calls stay off until you opt in again.
