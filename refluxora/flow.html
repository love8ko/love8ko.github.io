My RSI just came back at 18. Is that bad enough to see an ENT this week?
● Grounded · 3 sources
An RSI of 18 is above the Belafsky 13 threshold — your ENT visit is justified. The 9-question RSI is a screening tool from Belafsky 2002[c1], validated for detecting laryngopharyngeal reflux. Scores ≥13 correlate with abnormal pH-impedance findings in ~75% of cases[c2]. AAO-HNS recommends laryngoscopy + reflux finding score before empiric PPI trials at this severity[c3].
Educational — confirm with your clinician.
⚠ RSI is a screening tool, not a diagnostic test. Laryngoscopy is needed to confirm LPR.
