🔒 Lock decision — A Cool Relief (2026-05-23)

Founder approved A Cool Relief after reviewing Sprint 1 workbench. The recommendation order presented для founder was:

1.A · Cool Relief — best cross-cohort fit, calming = on-brief, mint differentiates от NoBurn teal. Safe pick that doesn't over-rotate to one persona. ⭐ Default recommendation.
2.D · Bold Modern — best for premium positioning (Voquezna era, LPR Pro $39.99/yr). Most differentiated и most "Year-2-ready" if Larry + Voquezna become the wedge ($5.5M best-case scenario).
3.B · Warm Foodie — best for Frank/Anna funnel. Risk: feels "too lifestyle" when Brian or Larry land on the same paywall.
4.C · Pregnancy Soft — wonderful for Patricia (12% of audience), но Brian (25%) и Larry (15%) — 40% of audience — wouldn't feel urgency for severe-pain или career-stake situations.
