2. What an alarm app can and can't do

  • Morno schedules alarms through Apple's AlarmKit. Whether an alarm rings also depends on your iPhone: it must be on and charged, Morno must be allowed to schedule alarms, and the volume, Focus and iOS settings must allow it. iOS updates and device problems can affect alarms.
  • iPhone keeps a Stop button on every alarm, and no app can remove it. Morno's missions, check again and backup alarms (every 2 minutes, up to 10 times) make it harder to go back to sleep, but they cannot make it impossible.
  • Photo recognition, rep counting and step counting can make mistakes, for example in a dark room. Every mission has another way to finish it.
  • For anything you can't afford to miss (a flight, an exam, a shift), also set an alarm in the Clock app or another device. To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for a missed alarm, a late arrival or its consequences.

3. Exercise and safety

Choose push-ups or squats only if exercise is safe for you, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath. Right after waking up you may be unsteady: get up slowly, turn on a light, and keep the path to your spot clear. Place your phone on a stable surface for exercise missions. Pick the 30-step or photo mission if exercise isn't right for you.
