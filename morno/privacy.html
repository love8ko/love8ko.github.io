Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Morno ("the App") is an alarm clock for heavy sleepers. It is developed and published by Valerii Loveiko ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the App keeps on your iPhone, what it sends to outside services and why, how long anything is kept and the choices you have.

Summary. No account, no ads, no tracking, no AI. Your alarms, missions, the photo of your spot, step counts and your morning history stay on your iPhone; we cannot see them. To run your subscription, Apple and RevenueCat receive your purchase under a random ID, together with the dates of a few steps in the App (for example, when you finished setup). We do not sell or share your data.

1. Data stored only on your iPhone

  • Your alarms and settings: alarm times and days, missions, sounds, Big mornings, skips, theme.
  • The photo of your spot. When you set up a photo mission, the App takes one photo of the place or object you choose (for example, your coffee machine). It keeps a small copy of that photo (about 480 pixels) and a numeric "fingerprint" of it (an image feature print made by Apple's Vision framework on your iPhone), plus up to five more fingerprints from successful mornings so recognition keeps working. Each morning the new photo is compared with these on your iPhone; morning photos are not saved. The photo is deleted when you delete the mission (if no other mission uses that spot) or the App.
  • Push-ups and squats. The front camera finds the position of your body on your iPhone to count reps. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.
  • Steps. For the walking mission and the check again, the App reads the step count from your iPhone's motion sensor (Motion & Fitness). Step counts are not sent anywhere.
  • Your mornings: when each alarm rang, when you finished your mission and the check again, and test runs. This is what the Mornings tab shows.
  • Technical logs of alarm scheduling and of test runs (for example, how close a photo match was), used only on your iPhone to show results and fix problems. In test builds (TestFlight) you can choose to send the test log to us yourself; it contains no photos.
  • Your next alarm is shared with the App's widgets through an App Group on your iPhone.

Your device backups (iCloud or computer) may include this data, depending on your Apple settings. Deleting the App removes everything it stored on your iPhone.

2. Data sent to outside services

2.1 Purchases: Apple and RevenueCat

Subscriptions are processed by Apple; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The App uses RevenueCat to know whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat creates a random ID for your installation of the App and receives purchase information (product, dates, trial and renewal status) and technical details such as device model, iOS version, App version, language and country or region.

With the same random ID, the App also sends RevenueCat the date of each of these steps, once: trial started, setup finished, first test alarm (with its result, for example "ring confirmed"), first morning (with its result), App opened on day 3, and paid. We use these dates to see where people get stuck and to improve the App. They contain no photos, step counts, alarm times or anything you typed.

Apple and RevenueCat process this data under their own privacy policies. We do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2.2 Email to us

If you tap "Write to us", your mail app opens a message to us with the App version, iOS version, language setting and the random purchase ID filled in, so we can help. You can delete these lines before sending. We keep support emails only as long as needed to answer and follow up.

3. What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No tracking and no data brokers. We do not sell or "share" personal information (as defined in California law).
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs besides the purchase information above.
  • No AI services. No location. No contacts. No health records.
  • No account and no login.

5. How long we keep data

Data on your iPhone stays until you delete it or the App. Purchase information and the step dates in section 2.1 are kept by RevenueCat while your subscription history is needed for the App to work and for accounting, and as described in RevenueCat's policy. You can ask us to delete them at any time (section 6).

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete a mission to delete its photo; delete the App to delete everything on your iPhone.
  • Turn camera or motion access off at any time in iOS Settings → Morno.
  • Ask us to access or delete the purchase data linked to your random ID: email loveykovl@gmail.com from Settings → Write to us (it includes the ID). We answer within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, in California or the European Economic Area), you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete or object to processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. We will not treat you differently for using them.

Legal bases (EEA/UK): performance of our contract with you for the subscription, and our legitimate interest in improving the App for the step dates in section 2.1.

7. Children

Morno is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Security and transfers

Data on your iPhone is protected by iOS. RevenueCat and Apple may process purchase information in the United States and other countries under their safeguards.

9. Changes

If we change what the App collects or sends, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the App.
