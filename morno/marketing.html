Morno

The alarm clock for heavy sleepers

Sleep through alarms, or turn them off half-asleep? Morno gets you out of bed for a short mission, then checks again 10 minutes later. Rings even in Silent mode and Focus.

iPhone · iOS 26 or later · 7-day free trial on the weekly plan

How it works

1

Tonight: pick a mission and test it

A photo of your coffee machine, kettle or bathroom, push-ups, squats or 30 steps. A real test alarm rings in a minute, so you know it works.

2

Morning: it rings, you get up

Your alarm rings even in Silent mode and Focus. Stopping it doesn't end your morning: backup alarms ring every 2 minutes, up to 10 times, until you finish your mission out of bed.

3

10 minutes later: check again

Walk 30 steps to show you're still up. Went back to bed? The backup alarms come back.

What's inside

Missions that get you moving

Photo of your spot, compared on your iPhone. Push-ups and squats counted by the camera. 30 steps without a camera. Chain up to three.

Big mornings

Flight, exam, interview or first shift: Morno shows when to wake and when to leave, with one extra backup alarm and a full second check.

Sounds that build

12 alarm sounds in 4 packs, from Heavy sleeper to Roommate-friendly.

On your Lock Screen

Widgets for the Lock Screen and StandBy, a live countdown between checks and Control Center buttons.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking, no AI. The photo of your spot and your step counts stay on your iPhone. Subscriptions run through Apple and RevenueCat under a random ID. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Honest about the Stop button

iPhone keeps a Stop button on every alarm, and no app can remove it. Morno doesn't pretend otherwise: if you stop the alarm before your mission, the backup alarms keep ringing until you finish it. Morno is an alarm clock, not a medical device or a treatment for any sleep condition.
