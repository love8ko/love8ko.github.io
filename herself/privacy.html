2. What we collect and why

We collect only what is needed to make your scenes. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.

Your answers

You tell us what you want to change and describe a moment: where, when, who, small details, one step you'll take, and the tone (calm or bold). You may pick thoughts from your inner critic, write a short note (up to 400 characters), and answer a weekly check-in (up to 200 characters). We send these to DeepSeek to write your scene. What you write can be personal. We use it only to make your scenes.

Your voice recordings

Before each chapter you read a short text aloud (15–30 seconds). The app first checks the recording on your iPhone: sound levels, background music, and whether the words match. This check uses Apple's on-device speech recognition and sends nothing anywhere. The recording is then sent through our server to ElevenLabs, which makes a temporary AI voice from it. That voice reads your scene. See the Voice data notice.

Scene text

The scene DeepSeek writes is kept on our server for a limited time so that ElevenLabs can read it aloud sentence by sentence. The text is sent to ElevenLabs for that purpose.

Install ID

The app creates a random install ID. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. We use it to count scene credits (one new scene per week) and to apply daily usage limits that protect the service from abuse.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We learn whether your subscription is active. We never receive your card details. [TO CONFIRM: whether a subscription service such as RevenueCat will process subscription status and an app user ID; if so, name it here and in section 4]

Your consent record

When you agree to the three confirmations, the app saves which version you agreed to, when, and each box separately. This record stays on your iPhone. [TO CONFIRM: whether a copy of the consent record is also kept on our server as evidence of written consent]

Safety check

If your answers mention self-harm or wanting to die, the app stops making the scene and shows crisis resources. We do not store that result.

3. What stays on your iPhone

  • Your finished scenes (audio and text) are saved only on your iPhone. They are excluded from iCloud backup.
  • Your raw recording is deleted from your iPhone right after the scene is made.
  • The app keeps a simple usage journal (for example, which screen you reached) on your iPhone only. It is not sent to us.
  • If you delete the app, the scenes on your iPhone are deleted with it.

5. Voice data notice

A voice recording and an AI voice made from it may count as biometric data under laws such as the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA), the Texas Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act (CUBI) and Washington's biometric law (RCW 19.375). We treat them that way. [TO CONFIRM by counsel: wording, applicability and any additional state requirements]

  • Purpose. Only to read your own scenes in an AI copy of your voice. Not to identify you, and not for any other use.
  • Written consent. We ask before your first recording, with a separate checkbox. Nothing is recorded without it. The consent covers each recording you make.
  • Who receives it. ElevenLabs only, as our processor, to make the voice.
  • Retention. The AI voice and the uploaded recording are deleted right after each scene is made. In any case, an automatic job deletes every AI voice older than 1 hour. The raw recording on your iPhone is deleted right after the scene is made.
  • No sale. We do not sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from your voice data.
  • No other disclosure, unless you ask us to, or the law or a court order requires it.
  • Protection. Sent over encrypted connections and handled with at least the care we use for other sensitive data.

This notice is our public retention and destruction policy for voice data.
