## Proof scope

[Harkin et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26479070/), DOI 10.1037/bul0000025, meta-analysis of 138 randomized studies, N=19,951. Progress-monitoring interventions improved goal attainment on average (d=.40). This supports a component approach, not Herself or AI-voice efficacy. The plaque states the distinction. No clinical app claims or fabricated customer proof.

Do not reuse the previous evidence document's interpretation of Payne 2021 as a test of recorded own voices: the primary abstract describes unfamiliar voices associated with self/friend/stranger. That paper is not used here. This round does not modify the app's separate Why section.
