- Opening1–3: keep selected assets exactly; already explain own-AI-voice differentiation, self-worth, and evening use. Additional sequence needs broader situations rather than redoing the first three.
- Frame4: visual interruption on cream paper, one number and a precise evidence topic; proof of a component, not a sweeping science claim. A concrete native step links evidence to use. Less personal than other frames (model affinity1.85), appropriate for a trust role rather than replacing the acquisition hook.
- Frame5: two adult hands/two mugs create a shared relationship scene without inventing a partner outcome. Ask-for-needs headline matches moment-preparation phrases. External quote makes the otherwise tiny personal line readable; broad download motivation is only moderate.
- Frame6: ordinary clothes and mirror, no appearance transformation or shame. Body deserves kindness supports self-worth/body cohorts; the headline is stronger than the extra quote in the text ablation. Retain one quote for visual mechanism, not multiple claims.
- Frame7: specific weekly job application chosen by her; app neither applies nor guarantees an offer. Enlarged actual card exposes an actionable difference from generic positivity. Same underlying native weekly-step UI as proof4, different photograph and hierarchy.
- Frame8: keys signal a life transition, not evidence that app grants a home. Actual wish-picker plus A new chapter choice provides the next action. Earlier confidence answer also visible; no fabricated selection or UI control. Strong fit for new-chapter cohort, weaker universal relevance.
