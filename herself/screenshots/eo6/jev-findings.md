## Author audit and decision

Publish requested B for review. Third phone visually belongs to the night palette. Four concrete chapter titles expose breadth across money/relationships/body/dreams without another marketing list; do not present a curated catalog. Library sacrifices prominent waveform in third frame; first frame retains the audio/AI voice mechanism. Example follows app typography, dark tokens and actual playable/rename capability, with simplified generated layout.

Headlines stop the scan; first frame voice mechanism explains the app; critic affinity and evening self-kindness stay intact. For work/self-worth, first two provide the main hook. Relationship/money/body/new-chapter cohorts gain explicit topics in third phone, but topic labels alone do not explain a distinct outcome. Skeptics still hesitate. Preserve one subtitle/one card; no added footer or badge. No sleep, therapy, income, relationship or body-change guarantee.

Full size: spellings and four chapter titles verified, all visible above external card. Daily phrase exists lower in UI asset but is hidden by the external card in final crop. 110px: headline/photo/phone visible, chapter words not legible. 180/220px: subtitle/card clearer; chapter microtext needs enlargement. Cards, phone geometry, faces and warm lamp unchanged. 8px review-webpage gap only. No actual Jev pixel assessment; no cross-round score comparison with different states.
