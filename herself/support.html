Support

Herself: Manifest Affirmations · Loveiko Labs

Herself is personal support in your own voice. You answer a couple of questions and read a short text aloud. Herself writes a one-minute scene of your life where what you want has already happened, and reads it to you in an AI copy of your voice. A new chapter comes every week.

Write to us

A question, an idea, or something broke — write to loveykovl@gmail.com. It goes straight to the person who makes Herself.

The easiest way is from the app: Settings → Write to us. That email already includes your app version and your install ID, so we can find your chapters and purchases without asking for anything else.

Cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your name (Apple ID) → Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Herself → Cancel Subscription.

You can also get there from Herself: Settings → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends so you aren't charged again. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore purchases

New iPhone, or reinstalled the app? Open Herself → Settings → Restore purchases. Sign in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Extra chapters you bought stay with the iPhone that bought them, and come back when you reinstall Herself on that iPhone. They can't be moved to another device.

Delete your data

Open Herself → Settings → Your voice & data:

  • Delete my scenes and recordings — removes your scenes, recordings and unfinished answers from this iPhone, and a live AI voice copy if there is one.
  • Withdraw consent — Herself stops recording and sending anything until you agree again.

Your AI voice copy is deleted automatically within an hour after each scene, and your words are deleted from our server within 24 hours. To delete the records we keep by install ID (credits and purchases), write to us from Settings → Write to us. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

If you're struggling

Herself is a self-practice tool. It is not therapy or medical care, and not a substitute for professional help.

If you're in crisis or thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 (US), or your local emergency number.
