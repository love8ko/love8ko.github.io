3. What Herself is

You answer a few questions and read a short text aloud. Herself writes a one-minute scene from your answers, using an AI provider, and reads it to you in an AI voice made from your recording. A new chapter is added each week.

Herself is a self-practice tool. It is not therapy or medical care, and not a substitute for professional help. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Talk to a qualified professional about your health.

If you're in crisis or thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 (US), or your local emergency number.
