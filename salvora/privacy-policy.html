Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Salvora: AI Eczema Flare Tracker ("Salvora" or "the App") is developed and published by Valeriy Loveiko. This notice explains the App's local records, optional AI and weather features, subscription services, and your choices.

Summary: Your journal is stored on your device. If you allow an AI feature, the photo or question and relevant context are sent to the service providers below. Subscription and abuse-prevention services also process a pseudonymous identifier and limited service records. Optional weather, where offered in your app version, sends rounded coordinates after you enable it. We do not sell your data or use it for cross-app advertising tracking. Provider processing does not mean that data is immediately deleted: see section 5.
Educational use: Salvora supports personal tracking and education. It does not diagnose a condition, prescribe treatment, or assign a clinical severity grade from a photograph. It is not a substitute for medical care.

1. Information Processed

1.1 Journal, symptoms and care records

You can record photos, dates, body areas, captions, itch and sleep observations, questionnaire answers, triggers, treatments and questions for a visit. These records and the App's history are stored locally. The weekly POEM questionnaire and your own photo rating are separate: a photo rating is a self-reported 0–10 number, not an AI-generated POEM score. The App may use your onboarding choices and saved observations to personalize what it displays.

If you use Coach, relevant context can include your selected profile or age band, symptom scores and trends, triggers, treatment records, and photo descriptions associated with your question. The App does not automatically send the entire journal on every request. However, a promise that health or treatment information never leaves your device would be inaccurate: relevant context is included in the AI request after consent.

1.2 Photos and images that may contain a face

The App accesses the camera or system photo picker when you choose to take or import a photo. A local copy is added to your journal. If you allow AI photo processing, a resized copy of the selected image, or two selected images for comparison, is transmitted for descriptive analysis. Choose which area to photograph and crop out identifying details when they are not needed.

A photo may show your face, a child's face, or other identifying features. Salvora does not use face recognition, identify people, authenticate users from faces, or create biometric identity templates. Image content can itself be identifying even though the App does not deliberately attach your name to the AI request.

1.3 Coach questions and source search

When you allow Coach and send a question, the service receives the question, recent conversation and relevant context described above. The question can also be sent to an embedding service to produce a numerical representation used to search the App's bundled reference library. The library search and any local search cache are on the device; generating a new query embedding can require an external request. Relevant reference passages are included in the prompt used to generate an answer. Conversation history and displayed results are saved locally.

1.4 Subscriptions and service identifiers

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat processes a system-generated app user identifier, purchase and subscription information to validate access and provide subscription reporting. The global API gateway receives that pseudonymous identifier to check access and enforce usage limits. Pseudonymous does not mean guaranteed anonymous: an identifier can link service activity to a subscription record. Salvora does not collect your payment-card details.

Weekly and annual subscriptions offer a seven-day free trial to eligible subscribers when Apple confirms eligibility. The exact offer, local price and renewal terms are shown before purchase. Trial eligibility is not guaranteed for every Apple ID.

1.5 Optional weather and location

Where your app version offers weather, it starts disabled. If you choose to enable it and allow location access, the App rounds coordinates to 0.1 degrees before storing or sending them to Open-Meteo for weather and humidity. This is approximate, city-area location; it is not an exact home address. The weather request does not include your journal, health records or RevenueCat identifier. The provider also receives normal connection metadata, such as an IP address. Denying location permission leaves weather unavailable and does not prevent local journal use under the App's access rules.

1.6 Support and reports

If you email us or choose to report an AI answer, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you decide to send. The report template can include your app user ID and an answer ID; it does not automatically attach the answer or health history. You can edit or cancel the email before sending it.

2. Why We Process Information

  • Keep your personal journal, comparisons and locally generated reports.
  • Generate an AI answer, photo description or comparison you choose to request.
  • Find relevant source passages and adapt answers to relevant saved context.
  • Validate purchases, restore access and understand subscription performance through RevenueCat.
  • Enforce service limits, moderate unsafe content, protect the service and handle support requests.
  • Retrieve weather only when you have enabled that optional feature.

Optional AI and location processing depend on your choices and permissions. Core subscription processing supports the service you purchase; security records support abuse prevention. We do not train our own models with your records, sell your personal information, operate advertising networks in the App, or use your data for cross-app advertising tracking. Third-party providers have their own data-use and retention terms; we do not promise the same processing controls for every provider.

4. Storage and Security

Photos, journal records, conversation history, preferences and local search data are stored using the device's app storage. Reports can be generated on the device and shared only through an action you choose. Your iCloud/device-backup settings can include local App data in backups. Copies you export, email or save elsewhere are separate from the App's storage.

Network requests use HTTPS. Provider API credentials are kept on the service side in the Release architecture. The global gateway is not configured to persist question, answer or image bodies in its quota database or application logs. It does process those bodies transiently to route and check the request. Hosting providers may process network and operational metadata under their own terms. No system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Retention

  • Local records: kept until you delete them in the App or remove the App's local data. Local caches and generated temporary files can remain until cache cleanup or removal of app storage. Device backups and copies you export must be managed separately.
  • Global quota and access records: the service uses a seven-day rolling usage window. During service requests, it removes older rolling records, daily counters older than eight calendar days, and access-cache entries more than seven days past their cache expiry. Cleanup is triggered by service activity, not a guaranteed deletion timer: records can remain stored longer while the service is inactive. These records contain service identifiers/counters, not saved question or image bodies.
  • OpenAI: its API documentation states that API data is not used for training by default and describes abuse-monitoring retention of up to 30 days for the endpoints used here, with stated exceptions and account-dependent controls. We have not verified Zero Data Retention for Salvora's account. See the linked API data controls for the scope and exceptions.
  • DeepSeek and Alibaba/Qwen: processing, retention and any provider data-use controls are governed by the applicable API/regional service terms and account configuration. We have not verified a fixed deletion period or a zero-retention arrangement for these Salvora integrations; we do not promise immediate deletion or a blanket no-training guarantee for all AI providers.
  • Open-Meteo: its published notice describes technical logs that can include coordinates and an IP address and are deleted after 90 days.
  • RevenueCat and Apple: purchase, entitlement and related service records are retained under their respective service/privacy terms and applicable obligations; deleting the App does not delete a transaction or cancel a subscription.
  • Support: messages and attachments you send are kept as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, security or applicable legal obligations. Contact us about access or deletion.

Withdrawing AI consent stops new requests. It does not recall a request already processed or automatically erase records held by a provider. Provider retention must not be described as identical to storage on your phone.

6. Your Choices and Data Requests

  • AI consent: Coach and photo AI ask for separate permission before their first use. Read this policy through the consent prompt before allowing the feature. You can decline it or withdraw AI consent in Settings. Allowing it again requires a new affirmative action.
  • Camera and photo access: control permissions in iPhone Settings. Using the system picker does not give Salvora unrestricted access to your entire library.
  • Weather: leave the feature disabled or deny/revoke location permission in iPhone Settings. No background location-tracking purpose is implemented.
  • Local deletion: delete individual entries or use Settings → Delete all my data to remove local journal/photos, trigger/treatment records, checks and conversations. That action keeps profile/setup preferences and subscription status. It does not erase provider records, previously exported files or backups.
  • Removal and backups: removing the App removes its app-container data under iOS rules. Manage device/iCloud backups and copies outside the App separately. Apple/RevenueCat purchase records and any identifier retained by platform storage are not guaranteed to disappear on uninstall.
  • Subscriptions: manage/cancel through Apple ID subscription settings; uninstalling is not cancellation. In versions that provide the saved archive after expiry, existing records and local export remain available, while new observations and AI require renewed access.
  • Access, correction and deletion requests: contact us at the address below. We can help locate service records and coordinate applicable provider requests. We may need a pseudonymous app identifier or purchase reference to locate a record and verify the request. Do not send a password or payment-card number. Rights and any retention exceptions depend on applicable law.

Where applicable, you may also request portability, restriction of processing or object to processing, withdraw consent for future optional processing, and complain to your data-protection authority. We do not represent that deleting the App alone exercises every right concerning third-party records.

7. Adults and Caregiver Use

Salvora is intended to be operated by adults aged 18 or older, as stated in the consent screen. A parent or authorized caregiver may keep a child's skin records. The adult must have authority to provide that information and consent to any optional AI processing. Child records and photos can contain personal or health information even without a separate child account. Avoid unnecessary names, faces and other identifying details in AI requests.

8. International Processing

The providers above operate internationally. Depending on the service and route, information may be processed outside your country, including in the United States or mainland China. The preserved Alibaba AI route uses Beijing infrastructure. We do not promise that all information remains in your country or that there is no international processing. Applicable provider agreements, account settings and regional legal requirements determine the processing arrangements. Contact us with questions about your information or the available options.

9. Medical and Automated-Decision Limits

AI descriptions and answers may be incomplete or incorrect. Salvora does not provide a diagnosis, treatment decision or clinical severity grade from a photo. It does not make an automated decision about insurance, employment or access to medical care. Use the journal and sources as information to discuss with a qualified healthcare professional.
