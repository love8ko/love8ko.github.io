Fillsafe

PDF Form Filler · Scan, Autofill & Sign Forms

Open or photograph a form, fill it with the details you keep on your iPhone, check every field, sign with your finger and share one PDF. No account, no login.

How it works

  1. Open a form. Pick a common form, import a PDF from Files, or take a photo of the paper you were given.
  2. Fill it from your details. Names, dates and document numbers you saved — for you, your family, children or your company — go into the right boxes. Type or say the rest.
  3. Check and sign. See every answer on the page, fix anything, and sign with your finger.
  4. Share one PDF. Send it by mail or message, save it, or print it. Saved forms stay ready to reuse.

Your details stay on your iPhone

  • Profiles, identity documents, answers and signatures are stored encrypted on your iPhone and are never sent to AI.
  • AI helps read public blank forms; the values are filled in on your iPhone.
  • Photo restore is optional, part of the paid plan and asks its own consent: only the page you choose is sent to draw an empty copy.
  • No ads, no tracking. iCloud backup is optional and off by default.
