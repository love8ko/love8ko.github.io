Privacy Policy

BumpCheck: Pregnancy Ingredient Reference

Effective April 13, 2026. Updated November 14, 2026 for version 2.1 and existing installations.

Summary: Your saved checks, questions, notes and due date are stored on your device. AI is optional and sends selected content, including your stage, to OpenAI through our Cloudflare proxy after permission. RevenueCat processes purchases and activation milestones. We do not sell data or use advertising tracking. Older 2.0 installations also send their selected stage as a RevenueCat analytics attribute; version 2.1 stops that transmission and requests removal of the old attribute when used.

1. Who operates BumpCheck

BumpCheck is provided by Valeriy Loveyko. Contact loveykovl@gmail.com about privacy or your data. BumpCheck is an educational ingredient reference, not a medical device, diagnosis, treatment or a substitute for your clinician.

2. Information on your device

Checks, ingredient lists, sources, favorites, saved questions and answers, notes, visit information, due date, stage preferences and consent choices are saved using SwiftData and UserDefaults. We do not operate a cloud synchronization service for these records. Your device backups may include app data under your Apple settings. Deleting local records does not automatically remove copies in device backups.

3. Optional AI processing

After you enable AI, OpenAI may receive the selected label image, ingredient text, product name/category, your selected stage, your question and up to six earlier question-and-answer pairs from that conversation. The entire saved shelf and a due-date field are not sent. Do not include names or unrelated personal details in questions or photographs. Pregnancy, postpartum and breastfeeding context can reveal sensitive health information.

Images are resized and encoded as JPEG before sending. The Cloudflare proxy checks access, constructs model instructions and relays the request. It does not deliberately save photo, prompt or conversation bodies. This does not mean that no data is processed or retained by any provider.

OpenAI API data is not used to train models unless the account explicitly opts in. Under its default API policy, abuse-monitoring logs may include content and be retained for up to 30 days, with legal and safety exceptions. Zero Data Retention has not been verified for BumpCheck; we do not promise it. See OpenAI API data controls and OpenAI Privacy Policy.

You can decline or withdraw AI permission in Settings. This stops new AI requests; it does not retract requests already sent. Your saved answers remain readable. Local reference lookup, on-device label reading and local records do not require AI permission. Some barcode lookups use the separate services below. Version 2.1 records the AI consent version, choice and time and requests permission again when its disclosure changes materially.

5. Usage data and advertising

We use RevenueCat purchase reports and activation milestones to understand trial activation and returns. Therefore, although we have no separate advertising analytics SDK, some usage information is processed off-device. We do not use these records for targeted advertising, sell them or share them with a data broker for advertising. We do not access the advertising identifier. Apple may process diagnostics under your device settings; we do not operate a separate developer crash-reporting SDK in this version.

7. Your choices, withdrawal and deletion

In Settings, turn AI off to stop new AI requests or delete local records. In version 2.1, local deletion also queues a request to remove the proxy's per-user quota/access-cache metadata; if offline, it retries on a later app opening. Shared network/global security counters are not reset by this action and expire under the retention schedule above. Earlier versions only delete local records.

Local deletion does not cancel a subscription, erase Apple/RevenueCat purchase history, remove device backups or guarantee immediate deletion of provider safety logs. Restore purchases remains available. To request access, correction or deletion of information held by a provider, contact us and identify the request without emailing sensitive product/health details unnecessarily. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply. We will explain the applicable steps and any legally required retention. A pseudonymous RevenueCat ID may persist through reinstall, so uninstalling is not a guarantee of a new service identity.

8. Children, security and medical context

BumpCheck is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. Do not submit a child's identifying details. We use HTTPS, provider credentials held on the server, access checks and bounded request quotas. No system can guarantee absolute security. Ingredient references and AI summaries are educational; discuss product or medication changes with a qualified clinician.

9. Changes and contact

We update this page when processing changes. Material AI disclosure changes also require renewed permission in the app. Questions: loveykovl@gmail.com.
